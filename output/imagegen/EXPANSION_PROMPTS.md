# Expansion image-generation prompt record

All bitmap masters were created with the built-in OpenAI image-generation and
editing path. Isolated sprites were generated against a flat chroma field and
converted locally to transparent RGBA PNG.

## Shared room master

> Hand-painted storybook / cozy anime illustration with soft warm brushwork.
> A 2.5D dollhouse cutaway diorama viewed from a high 3/4 overhead angle. Two
> back walls meet at a central corner. The room sits on a raised rounded wooden
> platform floating in a dark near-black plum background with soft vignette.
> Match the living-room reference lens, corner composition, and platform
> framing. Keep the floor largely open and readable. No characters, ghosts,
> text, or UI. Exact 16:9 composition.

Clean masters added the room-specific furniture and warm, inviting lighting.
Bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, attic, child room, and garden were generated as
separate clean masters using the existing living room as the style/composition
reference.

## Dirty-state edits

Each dirty room directly edited its matching clean master:

> Preserve the exact camera, crop, perspective, architecture, platform, and
> placement and size of every large or built-in object. Change only condition,
> surface treatment, lighting, and small loose details. Make the room
> cool/desaturated/dim with lights off, thick ambient dust, upper-corner
> cobwebs, cracked or peeling finishes, and faded fabrics. Do not add focal dirt
> piles, stains, trash, characters, ghosts, text, or UI.

## Sprite families

Tool icons used the existing broom as reference: glossy semi-realistic 45°
hero icons, upper-left light, soft shadow, saturated colors, and a lavender
accent.

Surface dirt used the existing floor stain as reference: flat top-down,
watercolor edges, no cast shadow, irregular shape. Grease, mold, leaves, and
weeds each received three deliberately distinct silhouettes.

Clutter used the existing book sprite as reference: one readable 3/4 object or
small cluster, soft contact shadow, cozy storybook realism, no text.

Memories used the existing family-photo item as reference: nostalgic warm tone,
3/4 view, soft contact shadow, and no readable writing.
